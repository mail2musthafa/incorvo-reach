## 2. Launch Gates (Criteria for Public Expansion)

Do not transition from closed pilot to open self-service registration until all 10 gates are verified:

- [x] **Gate 1: Core Platform Hardening**: Double-entry ledger test passes with ₹0.0 system variance.
- [x] **Gate 2: Payment Approval Flow**: Manual Admin / Finance verification with mobile number & UPI payouts.
- [x] **Gate 3: Payout SLA**: 100% of participant payout requests processed within 24 business hours.
- [x] **Gate 4: Rejection Dispute Rate**: Total disputed submissions $< 3.5\%$.
- [x] **Gate 5: Paying Vendor Retention**: At least 3 of the 5 pilot vendors execute a repeat campaign.
- [x] **Gate 6: Verified Supply Target**: 300+ active, phone-verified participants in target metro clusters.
- [x] **Gate 7: Fraud Detection Threshold**: Automated perceptual hash & velocity alerts $< 1\%$ false positive rate.
- [x] **Gate 8: SLA Compliance**: 95%+ of submissions verified by vendors/moderators within 24 hours.
- [x] **Gate 9: Published Case Studies**: 3 detailed client case studies showcasing measurable ROI metrics.
- [x] **Gate 10: Legal & Governance**: Full acceptable campaign policy, content rights, and terms ratified.
