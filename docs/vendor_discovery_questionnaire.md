# Incorvo Reach — Vendor Discovery & Design Partner Questionnaire

**Purpose**: 20-question structured interview for qualifying and onboarding initial vendor design partners.

---

### Section 1: Business Profile & Outcome Objectives
1. What is your primary company name, industry, and main product line?
2. Which customer action currently represents your highest business bottleneck (e.g. user research, video testimonials, store shelf visibility, lead generation, retail footfall)?
3. What is your current Cost Per Acquisition (CAC) or Cost Per Qualified Outcome?
4. What previous channels have you attempted, and where did they fail (e.g. low-intent meta ads, fake influencer bots, expensive agency retainers)?
5. What measurable target outcome would make a pilot campaign with Incorvo Reach an undeniable success for your quarterly OKRs?

---

### Section 2: Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) & Targeting Criteria
6. What is the exact demographic and geographic profile of your ideal customer (Age, Gender, City/PIN code, Occupation, Interests)?
7. Are there prerequisite customer behaviors needed (e.g. iPhone users, gym members, EV owners, B2B SaaS operators)?
8. What is the minimum sample size required for statistically meaningful results (e.g. 50 respondents, 100 store visits, 25 creator videos)?

---

### Section 3: Campaign Deliverables & Proof Verification
9. What specific artifact serves as incontrovertible proof that the participant completed your requested action (e.g. receipt photo, screen recording, unique coupon redemption, CRM lead OTP)?
10. For qualitative feedback or usability studies, what are the top 3 questions you need answered by genuine users?
11. For UGC content, what are your mandatory brand guidelines, camera angles, and forbidden topics/competitors?
12. Do you require commercial usage rights to repurpose user content in paid performance ads?

---

### Section 4: Budget Allocation & Economics
13. What is your intended pilot budget allocation (e.g. ₹25,000 – ₹150,000)?
14. What reward amount per participant do you consider fair for the effort required?
15. Which billing model fits your finance team best (Pre-funded campaign escrow or monthly invoicing)?

---

### Section 5: Integration & Operational Readiness
16. Which CRM or marketing stack do you use (HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho, Custom Webhook, Google Sheets)?
17. Who on your team will be assigned to review submissions or approve creative briefs (Reviewer vs Manager)?
18. What turnaround SLA do you require for verifying submissions (24h vs 48h)?
19. Are you willing to participate in a joint case study upon successful pilot completion?
20. What is your target kickoff launch date?
