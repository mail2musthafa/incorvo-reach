# Incorvo Reach — Participant Recruitment & Supply Verification Plan

**Operating Entity**: Incorvo Reach  
**Target Milestone**: Initial Pilot Cohort of 300–500 Verified Participants in Hyderabad and Metro Clusters.

---

## 1. Supply Cohort Distribution

To satisfy diverse campaign types without cold-start supply constraints, recruitment is segmented into 4 specialized supply panels:

| Participant Panel | Target Count | Primary Campaign Allocation | Key Skill & Verification Check |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Research & Usability Testers** | 150 Users | Product Usability, Screen Recordings, UX Studies | Microphone clarity check, English/Telugu/Hindi fluency |
| **Verified UGC Content Creators** | 50 Creators | Vertical Video, Taste Tests, Creative Demos | Camera gear audit (1080p60), portfolio submission |
| **Retail Field Auditors** | 100 Auditors | Store Shelf Audits, Mystery Visits, QR Check-in | GPS Geofence accuracy, smartphone camera quality |
| **Qualified Lead & Demo Testers** | 200 Users | B2B SaaS Discovery, FinTech Onboarding | Demographic & occupation verification, LinkedIn/Work Email |

---

## 2. Recruitment Channels (Honest Positioning)

* **Guiding Rule**: Recruit with absolute honesty. Position Incorvo Reach as a legitimate micro-consulting and product-testing platform, **not** an "easy get-rich-quick" scheme.
* **Campus & University Clubs**: Partner with engineering, business, and design student clubs across Hyderabad (Hitec City, Gachibowli, Begumpet).
* **Creator Communities**: Onboard nano/micro creators seeking commercial digital licensing agreements.
* **Professional Tech Slack/Discord Groups**: Recruit junior developers, QA engineers, and freelance consultants for B2B product usability studies.

---

## 3. Tier Progression & Trust Scoring

```text
[BRONZE: 0-2 Missions] → [SILVER: 3-10 Missions, >90% Rating] → [GOLD: 10+ Missions, >95% Rating] → [ENTERPRISE PANEL: Certified Specialist]
```

1. **Bronze**: Access to standard surveys, awareness videos, and local QR offers.
2. **Silver**: Unlocks private usability studies, store audits, and high-value lead campaigns.
3. **Gold**: Unlocks unmoderated screen recordings, product sampling shipments, and UGC briefs.
4. **Enterprise Certified Panel**: Top 5% participants with direct invitation to live moderated interviews (₹500–₹1,500/hr).

---

## 4. Identity & Payout Verification Flow

* **Phone Verification**: Mandatory SMS OTP verification before mission reservations.
* **Device & Fingerprint**: Single-account policy enforced via browser canvas & device hash.
* **Payout Account Verification**: Direct UPI VPA validation or IMPS/NEFT bank verification matching the registered user legal name.
